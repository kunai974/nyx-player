Changelog

All updates, fixes, and improvements to Nyx Player.

v0.8.1 Latest November 2, 2026

Fixed

  • Providers without TMDB links — Their movies and series now show on Home and in Search, with the provider's title and poster, and can be added to My list.
  • Malformed details — A title whose details arrive malformed from the provider (poster, category, number) is no longer dropped.
  • "Other" row — Titles filed under a category the provider does not list now appear in an "Other" row at the end of Home.
  • Next episode — The current and next episodes are found again with providers that write episode numbers differently, and the countdown pauses with the video.
  • More providers — Sign-in accepted by some providers that used to answer with a web page, clear messages for expired subscriptions and refused https certificates, pasted full addresses, and live channels in HLS when the account only allows it.
v0.8.0 November 1, 2026

Features

  • Android and Android TV — Nyx Player now runs on Android phones, Android TV and Chromecast with Google TV, with the same account and sync as on Windows.
  • Remote-friendly TV interface — Full remote navigation, a safe zone for TVs that crop the edges, 5-column "See all" pages and fast channel zapping.
  • Automatic source refresh — Your IPTV sources refresh on their own every 12 hours.
  • Channels-only providers — Providers that only offer live channels are now supported.

Improved

  • TV Guide — Only visible channels and hours are drawn, scrolling is smoother, and your selection is kept when the guide reloads.
  • Home screen — Smoother banner and rows, lighter startup.
  • Playback — Bounded retries, a clear "Playback interrupted" screen, subtitle and audio choices that carry over to the next episode, and HTTPS certificates are now verified.
  • Security and privacy — IPTV credentials no longer appear in error reports, less data is sent, and known network-library vulnerabilities are fixed.
  • Website — New home page in English and French, updated legal pages.

Fixed

  • Sync — Deleted items no longer come back, failed uploads are retried, and My List no longer loses titles when your provider changes.
  • Session — You stay signed in after the app stops and when the network comes back.
  • Sound — On Windows, sound is no longer stuck muted after a mute in a previous playback.
  • Account deletion — A deleted account is detected on your other devices.
v0.7.6 April 10, 2026

Fixed

  • Large Screen Layout — Fixed UI clipping and overlap on 1440p, 4K, and ultrawide displays, including Windows scaling (125%+).
  • Dashboard Skeleton Loading — Fixed missing skeleton placeholders on initial app launch, ensuring smooth visual feedback while content loads.
  • Hidden Categories Sync — Fixed an issue where hidden categories could reappear after logging back in.

Features

  • EPG (Electronic Program Guide) — See what's currently playing and coming up next on Live TV channels. Integrated into the player overlay, channel cards, and channel switcher. Auto-syncs at startup.
  • Hardware Acceleration Toggle — New option in Settings to enable or disable GPU acceleration on the fly for troubleshooting playback issues.

Improved

  • Security — General security improvements and hardening.
  • Performance — Various optimizations for smoother UI rendering and reduced resource usage.
v0.7.5 April 1, 2026

Fixed

  • Content Ordering — Channels, movies, series, and categories are now strictly displayed in the exact order provided by your IPTV source.
v0.7.4 March 30, 2026

Features

  • Official TMDB Logos — Dashboard hero banners and detail pages now dynamically display official beautiful logos for movies and series when available.
  • In-Player Channel Switcher — Browse categories and switch channels directly from the player without leaving your stream.
  • In-Player Episode Switcher — Navigate seasons and episodes from the player with TMDB-enriched thumbnails and titles.

Improved

  • Stretch Mode — Added a third aspect ratio option (Fit / Fill / Stretch) to perfectly accommodate ultra-wide content without cropping.
  • Binge Mode — The "Next Episode" popup now displays the episode thumbnail and title (TMDB or provider fallback).

  • Dashboard scroll positions and active modes are now preserved when returning from a movie or series page
