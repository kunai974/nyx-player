3. Service Description

Nyx Player is a player: it plays the channels, movies and series of an IPTV service that you choose and subscribe to with a third party (your "IPTV provider"), using the login details that provider gave you (Xtream Codes compatible servers).

The Application offers in particular:

  • live TV playback, with a program guide if you turn it on;
  • playback of the movies and series offered by your provider (video on demand);
  • enriched title pages (posters, synopses, ratings, trailers) based on TMDB;
  • "My List", resuming playback where you left off, favorite channels and hidden categories;
  • synchronization of these items and of your settings across your devices signed in to the same account.

What stays on your device. Your IPTV provider's login details (server address, username, password) are stored encrypted on your device and are never sent to our servers, except encrypted for your TV only if you connect it with your phone (we cannot read them). They are therefore not synchronized: on a new device, you need to enter them again. Your provider's catalog is also kept on the device. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Supported devices. The Application is available for Windows, for Android phones and for Android TV devices (for example Chromecast with Google TV or Android TV boxes); the same Android app works on the phone and on the TV. Some features may differ from one device to another. Only download the Application from the official website nyxplayer.app: we cannot vouch for a copy obtained elsewhere.

Updates. On Windows, the Application checks for new versions itself and offers to install them. On Android, new versions are downloaded from nyxplayer.app. An old version may stop working properly with our services; we recommend using the latest version.

Price. The Application is currently free and contains no advertising.
