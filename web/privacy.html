1. Who We Are

Nyx Player (the "Application") is a video player for IPTV services, available on Windows, Android phones and Android TV. It is a personal project published by William Payet, an individual based in France (see the legal notices), who is the data controller and can be reached at contact@nyxplayer.app.

Nyx Player does not provide any TV channel, film or series. You connect it to the IPTV service of your choice (your "IPTV provider"), which is an independent third party.

2. What Stays on Your Device

Most of your data never leaves your device:

  • IPTV credentials (server address, username, password) — stored encrypted on your device: in the Windows Credential Manager on Windows, and with a non-exportable Android Keystore key (AES-256-GCM) on Android. They are never sent to our servers.
  • Local database — your provider's catalog (channels, films, series), the TV guide if you enable it, your watchlist, playback progress, favorites and settings, in a SQLite database on your device (one per account).
  • Android backups — the Android app excludes its data from Google backups and from device-to-device transfers.

3. Data We Collect

Nyx Player can only be used with an account. Your email address and a password are required to create one; without them the Application cannot be used. The following data is stored in our cloud database to synchronize your devices:

  • Account — email address and password (the password is stored hashed by our authentication provider; we never see it)
  • Profile — profile name and color
  • Watchlist — titles you saved (TMDB identifier, type, identifier in your provider's catalog, date added)
  • Playback progress — for content you started watching: title, poster address when it comes from TMDB, TMDB identifier, identifiers in your provider's catalog (film, series, next episode), season and episode, position, duration, file format and date
  • Live TV — favorite channels and pinned categories
  • Hidden categories — categories of films, series or channels you chose to hide
  • Settings — the Application's settings, for example language, home page, display (chosen rows and genres, subtitle size), playback and TV guide options, chosen DNS resolver
  • Provider fingerprint — a fingerprint (SHA-256 hash) of your IPTV provider's server address, used to detect a change of provider. It is also stored with your playback progress, favorite channels and hidden categories, to know which provider each item belongs to. The address itself is not sent, but a known address can be matched to this fingerprint.
  • Usage data — app version, operating system and the date of the last launch, updated each time the Application starts while you are signed in (internal statistics only)
  • Technical data — IP address, device or browser information, email address, account identifier and the requests made (for example the titles searched through our TMDB server), recorded by our authentication service (with each signed-in session) and in the logs of our servers (security and abuse prevention)

We do not collect your IPTV credentials, your viewing history of live channels, your location, contacts, advertising identifiers or biometric data. The Application contains no advertising and no advertising tracker. The address of your provider's server is not collected: your provider's posters stay on your device, and this address is removed from crash reports (see section 4). Previous versions of the Application also sent the address of your provider's posters; it is replaced the next time the playback progress concerned is synchronized.

4. Crash Reports and Bug Reports

The Windows version of the Application (and any other version built with crash reporting) sends crash reports to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) so that we can fix errors. A report contains the error, the technical context (operating system, browser engine, language and time zone) and the last technical events that led to it (up to 100: screens and actions in the app, network requests; a request to our services, for example to TMDB, may show the title being viewed). Before sending, the Application removes from the whole report login credentials (username, password), the address of your provider's server and the path of its addresses (stream number), address parameters, tokens, your account identifier, email addresses and IPv4 addresses; it also asks Sentry not to record your IP address. Reports are not linked to your account.

These versions also send Sentry, with the same context and the same scrubbing, short diagnostic messages when signing in to your account runs into a problem (for example a session that expired or was refused by the server), with the error code and the age of the session.

If you use the "Report an issue" form in Settings → About, the text you write, the app version and the same technical context as a crash report are sent to Sentry. Do not include personal data or your IPTV address in it.

5. Why We Use It and Legal Basis

  • Providing the service (account, synchronization between your devices, settings) — performance of the contract you accept when creating an account (Terms of Service)
  • Security and fixing errors (crash reports, technical logs, abuse prevention) — our legitimate interest in keeping the Application reliable and secure
  • Usage statistics (active versions and platforms) — our legitimate interest in knowing which versions are still in use
  • Website audience and display (see section 14) — our legitimate interest in running and improving the website

Your data is never sold, never used for advertising and never used for profiling. No decision is made about you by automated means.

6. Where Your Data Is Hosted

Cloud data is stored by Supabase (PostgreSQL database and authentication) on Amazon Web Services servers located in the United States (region us-east-1, North Virginia).

7. Service Providers

The following providers process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase Inc. — authentication, cloud database, server functions (United States)
  • Resend Inc. — sending account emails: confirmation, password reset, email change (United States)
  • Functional Software, Inc. (Sentry) — crash and bug reports (United States)
  • Vercel Inc. — hosting of the nyxplayer.app website, of the account pages and of Windows update information, and anonymous website statistics (United States)
  • Cloudflare, Inc. — hosting of the Windows update files (United States)

8. Other Services the Application Connects To

To work, the Application connects directly to the services below. Like any website, they can see your IP address and the request made; we do not send them your account data.

  • Your IPTV provider — receives your credentials and your viewing requests, and the Application loads the images of its catalog (logos, posters), which may be hosted by third parties chosen by your provider. That is how the service works: its own privacy policy applies.
  • The Movie Database (TMDB) — descriptions and ratings are fetched through our server (TMDB receives the titles of your catalog to search for, but not your identity or IP address; our server keeps the results in a shared cache that is not linked to your account); posters are loaded directly from TMDB.
  • YouTube — trailer thumbnails; a trailer opens in your browser.
  • GitHub — channel logos (tv-logo project).
  • epgshare01.online — the TV guide, only if you enable it.
  • Cloudflare or Quad9 — secure DNS (DNS over HTTPS), used to find the address of your provider's server and of the TV guide. Cloudflare is used by default; you can choose Quad9 or your system's DNS in Settings → General → Network. If the chosen resolver does not answer, the other one is tried, then your system's DNS. Video playback always uses your system's DNS.
  • nyxplayer.app and releases.nyxplayer.app — checking for and downloading updates on Windows.

9. Security

  • Exchanges with our services and providers (Supabase, Sentry, nyxplayer.app, releases.nyxplayer.app) and with TMDB are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Each account can only read and modify its own data (Row Level Security on every cloud table).
  • IPTV credentials are encrypted on your device (see section 2).

Important: many IPTV providers use unencrypted connections (http://). In that case your credentials and streams travel in clear text between your device and your provider. This depends on your provider, not on Nyx Player.

10. How Long We Keep Data

  • Accounts not confirmed by email are deleted after 48 hours.
  • Account data is kept as long as your account exists.
  • Signed-in sessions (with the IP address and device information recorded at sign-in) are kept until you sign out or delete your account, and for at most 90 days without use: a device left unused that long will ask you to sign in again.
  • Deleting your account (Settings → Account → Delete my account) permanently deletes your account and all its cloud data, as well as the data of that account on the device you use. Security logs of the authentication service may remain after deletion, for at most 90 days.
  • Signing out does not delete data from the device: your local database and credentials stay there so you can sign in again quickly. To erase them for the signed-in account, use Settings → Data & Cache → Full Reset (which can also delete your list, progress, favorites and settings from the cloud). On Android, uninstalling the Application also erases everything. On Windows, uninstalling may leave saved credentials and data on the computer: use Full Reset first.
  • Crash reports and server logs are kept for a limited time set by the provider concerned (Sentry, Supabase), then deleted automatically. The security log of the authentication service is deleted after 90 days.

11. Transfers Outside the European Union

Our cloud data is hosted in the United States, and several providers listed above are established in the United States or the United Kingdom. These transfers rely on the safeguards offered by these providers: the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and, where the provider is certified, the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework. The United Kingdom benefits from an adequacy decision of the European Commission. You can obtain a copy of these safeguards by writing to contact@nyxplayer.app.

12. Your Rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to:

  • access your data and receive a copy of it;
  • rectify inaccurate data;
  • erase your data — you can delete your account directly from the Application (Settings → Account → Delete my account);
  • portability — receive your data in a structured format (JSON), on request by email;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interest, and restrict processing.

To exercise these rights, write to contact@nyxplayer.app. We answer within one month.

13. Minimum Age

Nyx Player is intended for people aged 15 or over. If you are under 15, please do not create an account. If we learn that an account was created by someone under 15, we delete it together with the data linked to it. A parent who believes their child has created an account can write to us at contact@nyxplayer.app.

14. Cookies and Website

The Application places no cookies; its settings are stored locally on your device. The nyxplayer.app website uses no advertising cookies. It measures its audience with Vercel Web Analytics, which collects anonymous, aggregated statistics (pages viewed, country, browser type) without cookies and without following you across other websites. Its fonts are hosted on the website itself: no external font service is contacted.

15. Changes and Contact

We will update this page when the Application changes the way it processes data; the date at the top shows the latest version. For any question about your data: contact@nyxplayer.app.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can file a complaint with the CNIL (French Data Protection Authority).
